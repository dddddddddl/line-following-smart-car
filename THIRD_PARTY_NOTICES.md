# Third-party notices and provenance

## Adafruit NeoPixel

`firmware/line_following_car/Adafruit_NeoPixel.cpp` and `.h` are the third-party files supplied in the project archive, kept byte for byte. Their existing authorship and GNU LGPL v3-or-later notices are retained.

The source credits Phil Burgess / Paint Your Dragon for Adafruit Industries, PJRC, Michael Miller and other contributors. The complete [LGPL v3 text](third_party/LGPL-3.0.txt) and accompanying [GPL v3 text](third_party/GPL-3.0.txt) are included. These files are not attributed to the project team.

Upstream project: https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_NeoPixel

## Educational platform and support files

The SmartCar manual identifies the SmartELab / Wuhan University electronic technology teaching team. The supplied `comm` and `motor` utilities accompany that educational platform. Their exact revision and per-file author allocation were not supplied, so this repository does not claim exclusive authorship over them.

## Portfolio preparation

The archive's controller and support files were organized into one Arduino sketch directory. English comments, portable `Arduino.h` casing, a Nano PlatformIO configuration, documentation and focused host checks were added. The supplied control logic and tuning were retained.

The raw coursework report, student identifiers, signature/contribution pages and kit manual are not published. Team work is described collectively without a per-person contribution table. No repository-wide open-source license is assigned without a confirmed basis.
